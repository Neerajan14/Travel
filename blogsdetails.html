Annapurna Circuit Trek Cost 2026: Full Budget Guide

Written by the Seven Peak Expedition team, based on our own guided departures on this route.

Planning the Annupurna Circult and trying to figure out what it actually costs? You are not alone. This is one of the asked questions Seven Peak Expendition gets from trekkers. The honest answer is that the trek can cost anywhere from $800 over $2000 depending on how you travel it.

Quick Answer
A guided Annapurna Circuit package typically costs $1,200 to $1,800 per person. Seven Peak Expendition's Annapurna Circuit Trek packages starts from $1.345 per adult for the full 17 to 18 day route, including permits, guide, accommodation, and meals on the trails.

If you are trekking independently without a package, budget roughly $900 to $1,300, plus your own time spent arranging permits, guides, and transport.

Trek through the foothills of the Annapurna region and immerse yourself in Nepal's natural beauty. Meet friendly locals, enjoy authentic Nepali cuisine, and witness unforgettable Himalayan sunrises.

End your trip by exploring local markets and enjoying traditional Nepali food before heading home with memories to cherish forever.
