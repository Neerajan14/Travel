Tour Overview

Experience the grandeur of the world’s highest peak in a single, unforgettable morning. Begin your journey soaring deep into the Himalayas, arriving as the breathtaking sunrise paints Mount Everest, Lhotse, and Nuptse in brilliant shades of gold. Step foot onto the iconic landing grounds of Kala Patthar, take in the crisp alpine air right beside Everest Base Camp, and celebrate your journey with a premium breakfast overlooking the highest peaks on Earth.

Tour Highlights

  • Achieve the ultimate mountaineering milestone—standing on the 8,848.86m summit of Mount Everest.
  • One-on-one private elite Sherpa guidance providing world-class technical support at every camp.
  • State-of-the-art oxygen systems and comprehensive medical logistics for maximum safety.
  • Thorough, professional acclimatization schedule designed by veteran Himalayan expedition leaders.
  • The pinnacle of human endurance, elite high-altitude camaraderie, and unmatched global views.

Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Kathmandu & Trip Briefing

Land at Tribhuvan International Airport and transfer to your luxury hotel. In the evening, meet your expedition lead and high-altitude pilot for a comprehensive safety briefing, equipment check, and weather optimization layout.

Day 2: Ultimate Everest Helicopter Expedition & Kala Patthar Landing

Board your early morning helicopter charter. Soar past giant Himalayan massifs, land at the legendary Kala Patthar (5,545m) for direct views of Everest, and celebrate your journey with a premium multi-course breakfast at the highest luxury resort in the world before flying back.

Day 3: Departure or Optional Cultural Add-On

Enjoy a relaxed morning in Kathmandu. Choose to explore historic local heritage sites like Boudhanath Stupa or Swayambhunath temple.
