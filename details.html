Tour Overview

Experience the magic of Pokhara in a single, unforgettable day. Begin your morning with a breathtaking sunrise from Sarangkot, where the first rays of light paint the Annapurna and Machhapuchhre peaks.

Tour Highlights

  • Enjoy an exhilarating tandem paragliding flight over Pokhara and Phewa Lake.
  • Lakeside breakfast at a cozy spot with stunning views.
  • Scenic boat ride on Phewa Lake, gliding across tranquil waters.
  • Professional guides and pilots ensuring a safe experience.
  • Perfect combination of adventure, relaxation and natural beauty.
