Tour Overview

Experience the grandeur of the world’s highest peak in a single, unforgettable morning. Begin your journey soaring deep into the Himalayas, arriving as the breathtaking sunrise paints Mount Everest, Lhotse, and Nuptse in brilliant shades of gold. Step foot onto the iconic landing grounds of Kala Patthar, take in the crisp alpine air right beside Everest Base Camp, and celebrate your journey with a premium breakfast overlooking the highest peaks on Earth.

Itinerary

Day 01: Arrival in Kathmandu(1,350m)

  • Activity: Airport pick-up by our Peak Expedition team
  • Details: Transfer to your hotel in Thamel. Evening briefing with your trekking guide regarding the trail conditions and gear check.
  • Overnight: Hotel in kathmandu

Day 02: Fly from Kathamandu to Lukla(2,800/9,186ft.), Trek to Phakding(2,610/8,563ft)

  • Take a scenic 30-minute flight from Kathmandu to Lukla, the gateway to the Everest region.
  • Begin trekking with a 3-4 hour descent(8km) along the Dudh Koshi River.
  • Cross suspension bridges and pass thought small Sherpa villages.
  • Admire the surrounding greenery and distant Himalayan views.
  • Highlights: Scenic flight, first glimpse of the Everest region, suspension bridges.
  • Trekking Time: 3-4 hours
  • Overnight: Teahouse in Phakding

Day 03: Trek from Phakding to Namche Bazaar(3,440m/11,283ft)

  • Trek 10.5km over 6-7 hours, crossing the Dudh Koshi River via suspension bridges.
  • Pass through villages like Monjo and enter Sagarmatha National Park.
  • Climb gradually to Namche Bazaar, the busting Sherpa trading hub.
  • Catch your first views Mt. Everest along the trails.
  • Highlights:Suspension bridges, Sagarmatha National Park, first Everest views.
  • Trekking Time:6-7 hours.
  • Overnight:Teahouse in Namhe Bazaar.

Day 04: Vist Hotel Everest View(3,880m) and Trek to Khujung Village(3,780m)

  • Trek 6.6km(3-4) to the Hotel Everst View for stunning panoramic views from Everest and surronding peaks.
  • Descend to khumjung village, a traditional Sherpa settlement.
  • Visit the Hilary School and khumjung Monastery, known for its purported yeti scalp.
  • Highlights: Hotel Everest View, khumjung Monastery, Hilary School.
  • Trekking Time:3-4 hours.
  • Overnight:Teahouse in Khumjung

Day 05: Trek from Khumjung to Pangboche(3,860m)

  • Trek 9km(5-6 hours) throught scenic Shrepa villages with breathtaking views of Ama Dablam, Everest, Lhotse, and Thamserku.
  • Cross suspension bridges and pass through Deboche before ascending to the traditional village of Pangboche.
  • Visit the historic Pangboche Monastery, the oldest monastery in the Everest region.
  • Highlights: Pangboche Monastery, Ama Dablam views, Sherpa Culture.
  • Trekking Time: 5-6 hours
  • Overnight:Teahouse in Pangboche.

Day 06: Trek from Tengboche to Dingboche (4,410m)

  • Trek 11km(5-6 hours), descending thrrought forests and crossing the Imja River.
  • Pass throuh Pangboche village and visit its ancient monastery.
  • Countine upward to Dingboche, nestled in a picturesque valley with views of Lhoste, Ama Dablam, and Island Peak.
  • Highlights: Imja River crossing, Pangboche Monastery, scenic valley views.
  • Trekking Time: 5-6 hoyrs.

Day 07: Acclimatization Day in Dingboche

  • Rest day in Dingboche (4,410m) to aid acclimatization before pushing higher.
  • Hike up to Nangkartshang Peak (5,083m) for panaramic views, then descend back to Dingboche.
  • Follow the "climb high, sleep low" rule to help your body adjust safely to the alitude.
  • Highlights: Nangkarshang Peak viewpoint, views of Makalu, Lhostse, Ama Dablam, and the Chukhung Valley.
  • Trekking Time: 5-6 hours.

Day 08: Trek from Dingboche to Lobuche (4,910m)

  • Trek 11km (5-6 hours), ascending gradually to Dughla
  • Climb steeply to Thukla Pass, passing memorials for climbers lost on Everest.
  • Countine to Lobuche, surrounded by majestic peaks like Nuptse.
  • Highlights:Thukla Pass Memorials, views of Nuptse and Lobuche peaks.
  • Trekking Time: 5-6 hours.
  • Overnight:Teahouse in Lobuche.

Day 09 : Trek from Lobuche to Gorak Shep (5,140m) and Visit Everest Base Camp (5,364m/17,594ft)

  • Trek 15km(9-10 hours) along the Khumbu Glacier's lateral moraine to Gorak Shep.
  • Countine to Everest Base Camp, admiring the Khumbu Ice fall and surrounding peaks.
  • Return to Gorak Shep for the night.
  • Highlights:Everest Base Camp, Khumbu Icefall, close-up Everest views.
  • Trekking Time: 9-10 hours.
  • Overnight:Teahouse in Gorak Shep.

Day 10 : Hike to Kala Patthar (5,545m) for Sunrise, Trek to Pheriche (4,280m)

  • Early morning hike to kala Patthar for sunrise views of Everest and surrounding peaks.
  • Descend to Gorak Shep for breakfast, then trek 13km (7-8 hours) to Pheriche.
  • Enjoy more comfortable conditions in Pherche for rest.
  • Highlights:Kala Patthar sunrises, Everest panoramic views, descent to Pheriche.
  • Trekking Time: 7-8 hours.
  • Overnight:Teahouse in Pheriche.

Day 11 : Trek from Pheriche to Namche Bazaar

  • Trek 20km (6-7 hours), retracing the trail through dramatic mountain and river views.
  • Enjoy the easier downhill walk back to Namche Bazaar.
  • Reflect on the adventure as you descend.
  • Highlights: Descending trail, mountain views, return to Namche.
  • Trekking Time: 6-7 hours.
  • Overnight: Teahouse in Namche Bazaar.

Day 12 : Trek from Namche Bazaar to Lukla

  • Trek 18km (6-7 hours) along the Dudh Koshi River, passing familiar villages.
  • Celebrate the completion of the trek upon arriving in Lukla.
  • Highlights: Return to Lukla, celebratory moments.
  • Trekking Time: 6-7 hours.
  • Overnight: Teahouse in Lukla.

Day 13 : Fly Back to Kathamndu from Lukla

  • Take a 30-minute morning flight back to kathmandu.
  • Relax and reflect on the incredible journey in Kathmandu.
  • Highlights: Scenic flight, time to relax in Kathamndu.
  • Flight Duration: 30 minutes.
  • Overnight: Hotel in Kathmandu.

Day 14: Departure

Transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu for your return flight home.

Trekking and Climbing Gears

Upper Body

  • One medium-weight polar fleece pullover
  • One polar fleece jacket
  • One Gore-Tex waterproof, breathable jacket with large hood (fits climbing helmet)
  • Lightweight down jacket for chilly base camp days or short breaks
  • Very warm goose-down (duvet) jacket with hood or down/duvet suit for high altitude
Note: Keep clothing dry using waterproof stuff sacks, bin liners, or large plastic bags.

Hands

  • One pair lightweight poly-liner gloves (for tying knots, worn inside mitts)
  • One pair mittens: Gore-Tex over mitt with warm polar fleece liner

Headwear

  • Warm wool or synthetic hat covering ears
  • Balaclava
  • Scarf or neck sleeve
  • Face mask
  • Ball cap or brimmed sun cap
  • Glacier sunglasses with side shields
  • Optional: Ski goggles (light and dark lenses)
  • Bandana or head scarf (for dusty conditions)

Lower Body

  • One pair walking shorts
  • One pair walking trousers for trekking and camp
  • One pair polar fleece trousers
  • One pair Gore-Tex waterproof/breathable trousers or bibs with full side zips
  • One pair goose-down (duvet) trousers or bibs (optional)
  • One pair One-Sport Everest overboots or equivalent (with Aveolite liners)
  • One pair crampons, harness (Zumar set), 4 carabiners, and ice axe
  • One pair sturdy leather or synthetic (Gore-Tex) hiking boots with ankle support (for walk to advanced base camp)
  • One pair cross-trainers, running shoes, or sandals (for Kathmandu and camp)
  • One pair down booties (optional)
  • Two pairs medium-heavy poly or wool socks
  • Two pairs liner socks (polypropylene or wool)
  • Vapor barrier liner socks or plastic bread bags
  • Two pairs lightweight trekking socks (poly or wool)
  • Light wool or cotton socks (for town)

Rucksacks and Travel Bags

  • One medium rucksack (50–70 liters / 3000–4500 cubic inches, airline carry-on size)
  • Two large durable duffle kit bags (120 L / 7500 cubic inches) for clothing and equipment (suitable for pack animals or porters)
  • Small padlocks for duffle bags

Sleeping Gear

  • One down (duvet) sleeping bag rated to –30°C for high altitude
  • At high camp, you can sleep in your down clothing inside the sleeping bag for extra warmth
